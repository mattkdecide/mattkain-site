Early Observations

🏗️
Operating Model
Regional Layer Clearer Than Expected
APAC reporting lines and budget ownership are clearer than expected at market level. The regional layer between Pete and global is now mapped. The key question is brief ownership vs. brief influence — who actually decides when markets disagree. That answer will shape how the account runs.
📐
Account Scope
Breadth vs Depth Tension Confirmed
The APAC mandate is wide — 5+ markets, three product lines, multiple agency relationships. Two weeks in, this tension is confirmed rather than resolved. Weeks 3–4 are about narrowing to the highest-leverage areas before forming a Phase 2 view.
🤝
Client Relationships
Pete & Will: Strong Start
Both Pete and Will have been engaged and open in early sessions. Pete is operating strategically; Will is execution-focused with genuine platform depth. The relationship is warm and the trust foundation is in place. Next step is demonstrating strategic value, not just listening.
🚀
Opportunity
COE Activation is the Unlocking Move
The Publicis / Zenith network has significant capability — Epsilon, Sapient, PMX — that appears underdeployed on this account. Getting the COE model activated is the highest-leverage move available in Phase 1. If the capability is there and not deployed, that is a commercial and relationship gap with a clear fix.
