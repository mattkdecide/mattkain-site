Context

The Renewal Moment

The Adobe APAC account reaches a natural renewal point in 2026 — the close of an initial three-year term and the opening of the next. Seven markets, active SOWs, established teams, working client relationships. What has developed is a collection of independent market operations rather than a cohesive, high-performing regional partnership. This is the moment to define what the next three years actually deliver.

The renewal will proceed — but the next 90 days is the window to codify the changes that should have been made, and the ambitions that have not yet been reached. The People and Client Operations work happening right now is not preparatory to the renewal. It is the renewal content. Whatever gets resolved across org design, ways of working, and accountability structures in the next 90 days gets written into the new three-year terms.

The plan is structured across three pillars — People, Client Operations, and Growth — each directly connected to one of the three Adobe.pub vision convictions. The sequencing is deliberate: build the team, build the relationship, then build the commercial case.

Landscape

Competitive Pressure & Strategic Response

Adobe’s competitive position is under real pressure from two directions simultaneously. The Vision positions Velocity Content and AI search optimisation as strategic responses to this shift, not peripheral add-ons.

Canva Moving Upmarket
Canva has captured the prosumer and SMB creative market with a speed and simplicity that legacy Creative Cloud cannot easily replicate — and it is moving upmarket fast into more professional and business workflows.
LLMs Commoditising Creative
Generative AI tools are commoditising basic creative and copywriting tasks that once required Adobe’s toolkit, lowering the barrier to entry across the content production stack.
Strategic Response
Agentic and AI-powered search is creating a new optimisation discipline at the intersection of SEO, content strategy, and model visibility. The next three years are a pivotal window for Adobe to defend its platform leadership in the enterprise and mid-market — and for Publicis to move decisively into the content supply chain layer before that territory is claimed by others. Velocity Content and Growth should be framed as audience intelligence, AI-enabled insight tooling, market support, content velocity, and enablement around Adobe’s evolving operating model.
Three Pillars

Vision Architecture

Each pillar connects to a lane. Everything in the 30/60/90 plan, the SOW, and the renewal conversation should ladder back to one of these three convictions.

Pillar 01 · People Lane
Culture & Destination
Become the account people at Zenith want to work on. Strong team identity, client integration, and meaningful recognition at every level. Elevate ICs closest to delivery into genuine client leaders with real ownership — not task lists. A destination account performs better, retains better, and attracts better talent.
→ People Lane
Pillar 02 · Client Ops Lane
Digital Excellence & Adobe Power
Be the best digital operator in the room. On the front foot with Lotame, LiveRamp, and Epsilon data partnerships. Best-in-class programmatic and SEM. Work that wins digital awards. Become the leading Adobe tool user in the Publicis network. Tie performance to at-risk compensation: when the account wins, the team wins.
→ Client Operations Lane
Pillar 03 · Growth Lane
Velocity Content & Growth
Expand the partnership beyond the media buy into content, creation, and optimisation. A Velocity Content team running rapid test-and-learn cycles across lower funnel media and social. Content strategy and production for SEO and AI-optimised search. More markets, more services — with a flexible resourcing model that allows Zenith to move quickly.
→ Growth Lane

SOW

SOW Implications

The Vision should be treated as the strategic front door to the SOW process. The SOW Wizard turns this into a scope review; this document explains why that review matters.

Organisational model and two-in-a-box engagement structure must be captured as operating commitments, not left as informal ways of working.
Digital excellence should translate into named capabilities, owners, cadence, tools, and measurable outputs.
Velocity Content, SEO extension, Agentic Search Optimisation, Adobe-on-Adobe, and creative/content production should be scoped as explicit growth services.
The renewal should include flexible resourcing language for emerging market opportunities, especially Philippines and Indonesia.
Any performance-linked team reward model needs commercial and HR review before being written into renewal mechanics.
